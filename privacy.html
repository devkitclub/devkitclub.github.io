Privacy

This page describes the DevKit Club static website. Last updated October 11, 2026.

What this website stores

The website has no accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. If you choose to reduce animations, it saves that preference in your browser’s local storage under devkit-reduce-motion. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Email updates

The signup button takes you to a MailerLite-hosted form. When you subscribe, DevKit Club uses MailerLite to manage your email address and subscription status and send updates about free tools, practical guides, and future paid kits. You can unsubscribe using the link in our marketing emails. Review the form’s consent information and MailerLite’s privacy policy before subscribing. This static website does not store your email address. Following GitHub releases does not subscribe you to our email list.

Hosting and external links

Your hosting provider may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to serve this website. Links to GitHub and MailerLite are subject to those services’ privacy practices. This website loads its images, fonts, scripts, and styles from its own files.

Questions

You can contact the project through RepoReady Lite’s GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not include personal information or secrets.
