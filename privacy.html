Privacy

This page describes the DevKit Club static website. Last updated October 10, 2026.

What this website stores

The website has no accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. If you choose to reduce animations, it saves that preference in your browser’s local storage under devkit-reduce-motion. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Email updates

This website does not collect or store email addresses itself. When email signup becomes available, the signup button will take you to a separate provider-hosted form. Review that form’s privacy and consent information before subscribing. Following GitHub releases does not subscribe you to a DevKit Club email list.

Hosting and external links

Your hosting provider may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to serve this website. Links to GitHub and any future email signup provider are subject to those services’ privacy practices. This website loads its images, fonts, scripts, and styles from its own files.

Questions

You can contact the project through RepoReady Lite’s GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not include personal information or secrets.
