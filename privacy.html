How DevKit Club handles browser preferences, email signups, and external links

Updated October 13, 2026

Browser preferences

This static website has no accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. Choosing reduced motion stores a preference in your browser under devkit-reduce-motion. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data

Email updates

Signup links open a MailerLite-hosted form. DevKit Club uses MailerLite to manage subscriptions and send product and free-tool updates. Review the form’s consent details and MailerLite’s privacy policy before subscribing

This website does not receive or store your email address. You can unsubscribe through the link in the emails you receive

Payments

No card details are collected here. Paid checkout is currently inactive. If checkout becomes available, it will open on Lemon Squeezy rather than process payment data on this website

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process technical request information to do so. Links to GitHub, MailerLite, and Lemon Squeezy are subject to those services’ own privacy practices. Fonts, scripts, and styles are served from this website’s files

Questions

See the support page for current help channels. Public GitHub issues should not contain personal information or secrets
